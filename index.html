Discover Batumi

Where your car can take you

The Adjara region is made for road trips — waterfalls, beaches and mountain villages, all within an easy drive.

💧

Makhuntseti Waterfall

A 30 m waterfall and a stone bridge from the era of Queen Tamar — about 30 km from Batumi.

🌿

Mirveti Waterfall

A hidden green gorge with a peaceful cascade — perfect for a summer escape.

🏖️

Sarpi Beach

Crystal-clear water at the Turkish border — one of the cleanest beaches on the coast.

🌅

Tsikhisdziri

Dramatic cliffs, sea views and the ruins of the Petra fortress just north of the city.

🏛️

Gonio Fortress

An ancient Roman fort and quiet pebble beach, 15 minutes south of Batumi.

🌴

Batumi Boulevard

7 km of seaside promenade, palm trees, fountains and cafés right in the heart of town.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about renting a car in Batumi with Lucky Rental Car.

Do I need a deposit to rent a car in Batumi?
No. Every car at Lucky Rental Car is rented with zero deposit — no money is blocked on your card.
What documents do I need?
A valid passport and driver's licence. The driver must be at least 21 years old with a minimum of 2 years' driving experience.
Do you deliver the car to the airport or my hotel?
Yes — delivery is free anywhere in Batumi, including the airport, your hotel or apartment. Delivery outside Batumi is available for a small additional fee.
Is insurance included in the price?
Yes. Every rental includes full CASCO insurance, so you're covered on the coast and in the mountains.
Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept USD, EUR, GEL and USDT — flexible options for travellers from anywhere in the world.
How do I book a car?
Just message us on WhatsApp or Telegram with your dates and preferred car. Your booking is confirmed once the dates are agreed and a prepayment is made.
