Discover Batumi

Where your car can take you

The Adjara region is made for road trips — waterfalls, beaches and mountain villages, all within an easy drive.

01

Makhuntseti Waterfall

A 30 m waterfall and a stone bridge from the era of Queen Tamar — about 30 km from Batumi.

02

Mirveti Waterfall

A hidden green gorge with a peaceful cascade — perfect for a summer escape.

03

Sarpi Beach

Crystal-clear water at the Turkish border — one of the cleanest beaches on the coast.

04

Tsikhisdziri

Dramatic cliffs, sea views and the ruins of the Petra fortress just north of the city.

05

Gonio Fortress

An ancient Roman fort and quiet pebble beach, 15 minutes south of Batumi.

06

Batumi Boulevard

7 km of seaside promenade, palm trees, fountains and cafés right in the heart of town.
